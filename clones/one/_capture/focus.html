Domains.

We work across six domains. In each, the task picks the float: what to observe, how often, and over how much ground.

Map the field. Watch the site. Cover the route.

  • Agriculture.

    Crop, soil and water conditions mapped field by field, season after season.

  • Security.

    Perimeters, gates and fence lines of fixed sites kept under regular observation.

  • Surveillance.

    Wide areas and long routes kept in view where fixed cameras cannot reach.

  • Survey.

    Land, boundaries and structures measured from the air and returned as maps.

  • Zoning.

    Land use, boundaries and change over time recorded for planning and permits.

  • Exploration.

    Remote and unmapped ground surveyed before anyone travels to it.
